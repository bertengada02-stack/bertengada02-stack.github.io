Keeply Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Keeply is a personal reminder and organization application developed by Oobert App Network. Keeply is designed to help users remember important things, dates, and reminders while keeping their information on their device.

Information You Enter in Keeply

Keeply allows you to enter and store information such as:

  • Names or descriptions of things you want to remember
  • Categories
  • Important dates
  • Reminder dates and times
  • Notes
  • Status and reminder-related information

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide Keeply's reminder and organization features.

Keeply does not transmit this information to Oobert App Network servers.

Data Collection and Sharing

Keeply does not require you to create an account.

The current version of Keeply does not collect or transmit your personal information to Oobert App Network servers.

Keeply does not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Keeply does not currently use advertising or analytics services.

Permissions

Keeply may use Android permissions and system capabilities necessary to provide reminder functionality, including:

  • Sending notifications
  • Scheduling reminders and alarms
  • Restoring scheduled reminders after the device restarts

These capabilities are used to provide Keeply's reminder features and are not used to collect personal information for Oobert App Network.

Device Storage and Backups

Keeply stores app information locally on your device.

Depending on your Android device, operating-system settings, and backup configuration, Keeply data may be included in Android backup or device-transfer functionality provided by your operating system or device manufacturer.

Oobert App Network does not operate a cloud storage service for Keeply user data in the current version of the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information entered into Keeply remains locally on your device until you edit or remove it through the app, clear Keeply's application data, or uninstall the application, subject to backup or restoration functionality provided by your device or operating system.

Keeply does not currently provide user accounts or store Keeply user data on Oobert App Network servers. Therefore, there is no Keeply server-side account or associated cloud data for Oobert App Network to delete.

Data Security

Keeply is designed to minimize the collection and transmission of personal information by storing your reminder information locally on your device.

Users should maintain appropriate security for their devices using the security features provided by Android and their device manufacturer.

Children's Privacy

Keeply is not specifically designed for or directed toward children. Keeply does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Oobert App Network servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Oobert App Network may update this Privacy Policy if Keeply's features or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be made available through Keeply and/or its Google Play listing, and the effective date above will be updated as appropriate.
